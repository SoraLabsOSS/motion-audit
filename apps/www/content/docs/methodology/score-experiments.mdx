## 1. Experimental Methodology

To evaluate the mathematical dynamics of browser rendering stages under controlled benchmark workloads, we construct synthetic test cases:

- **Controlled Isolation:** Altering a single variable at a time (property type, paint surface area, duration, concurrency, viewport size).
- **Observation Focus:** Recording the resulting numeric score ($0 - 100$) and assigned tier ($S, A, B, C, D, F$).
- **Disclaimers:** All observations reflect reproducible input/output mapping under benchmark conditions.

---

## 2. Test Cases and Observations

### Experiment 01: Property Cost Hierarchy

- **Payload A:** 10 elements animating `transform: translateX(100px)` (Compositor).
  - _Result:_ Score 98–100, Tier **S**.
- **Payload B:** 10 elements animating `background-color: #ff0000` over $100 \times 100\text{px}$ (Paint).
  - _Result:_ Score 88–92, Tier **A/B**.
- **Payload C:** 10 elements animating `width: 300px` (Layout/Reflow).
  - _Result:_ Score 62–68, Tier **C/D**.
- _SoraLabs Deduction:_ Layout modifications incur approximately $2\times$ the deduction penalty of paint operations, while compositor-only operations incur baseline negligible cost.

---

### Experiment 02: Paint Surface Scaling Law

- **Payload A:** Single element animating `box-shadow` on a small avatar ($40 \times 40\text{px}$, area ratio $\approx 0.001$).
  - _Result:_ Minor score deduction ($\approx -2$ points).
- **Payload B:** Single element animating `box-shadow` on a full-screen hero container ($1440 \times 900\text{px}$, area ratio $= 1.0$).
  - _Result:_ Severe score deduction ($\approx -22$ points).
- _SoraLabs Deduction:_ Paint penalties scale non-linearly with the surface area ratio ($\text{PaintArea} / \text{ViewportArea}$). Small micro-repaints are clamped to a minimum floor ($0.25\times$), whereas large repaints hit an amplification ceiling ($4.0\times$).

---

### Experiment 03: Duration and Micro-Interaction Discounting

- **Payload A:** Layout change lasting $50\text{ms}$ (instant state transition).
  - _Result:_ Moderate deduction ($\approx -5$ points).
- **Payload B:** Identical layout change lasting $1200\text{ms}$ continuous loop.
  - _Result:_ Severe continuous penalty ($\approx -20$ points).
- _SoraLabs Deduction:_ Under the Google RAIL performance framework, interactions under $100\text{ms}$ benefit from a duration discount factor, reflecting the cognitive threshold where transient frame pacing drops are perceived as instantaneous by the human visual system.

---

### Experiment 04: Consecutive Frame Thrashing Severity

- **Payload A:** 1 isolated read-after-write during initial document parsing and DOMContentLoaded.
  - _Result:_ Handled gracefully (Tier A/B).
- **Payload B:** Continuous read-after-write across $> 8$ consecutive frames during active rAF animation loops.
  - _Result:_ Instant drop to Tier **F**.
- _SoraLabs Deduction:_ Layout thrashing across consecutive animation frames locks the main thread across multiple 16.6ms frame budgets. Sustained consecutive frames act as an exponential trigger driving the audit score to failure.
